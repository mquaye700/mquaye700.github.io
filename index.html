My business journey

How I grew my business

Every step of my journey was shaped by learning, customer focus, and building something people could rely on.

Five years ago, I started with a single laptop, a sketchbook, and a clear ambition: to help small brands look professional online without sacrificing authenticity.

I built trust the old-fashioned way—through strong communication, open timelines, and by delivering more than expected. My first clients were nervous business owners with tight budgets.

As the work became more visible, I shifted from being reactive to thinking strategically. I started creating a clearer brand identity for myself, with a website that reflected the same quality I delivered to clients.
